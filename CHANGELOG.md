## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-13

### Added

- Lifecycle manager for servers, workers, and cleanup functions.
- Sequential reverse-order and parallel shutdown modes.
- Signal, context, component-exit, and timeout handling.
- Standard `net/http` adapter compatible with Gin and other frameworks.
- English and Traditional Chinese docs, runnable examples, tests, and CI.
